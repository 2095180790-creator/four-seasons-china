Things to do here

  • 01Golden Sunrise over the MountainsWake early and watch the first light turn the snow-covered peaks a deep golden orange.
  • 02Pilgrimage Walk in the Old QuarterWalk slowly through the historic streets, follow the local rhythm, and see the prayer flags in the wind.
  • 03Butter Tea on a RooftopWarm your hands around a cup of butter tea and look out over the winter rooftops toward the mountains.
